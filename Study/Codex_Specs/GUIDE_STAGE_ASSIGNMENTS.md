# Interview Guide Stage Assignments

Date: 2026-08-13
Status: Approved Session 2 authority

This document is the human-readable authority for stage-aware detailed interview guide selection. Production uses constants in `scripts/interview_stage.py`; production code must never parse this Markdown file at runtime. The registered smoke test parses the tables below and requires exact ordered parity with the executable constants.

The assignments select usable working subsets without deleting the complete rehearsal library. `all` remains the compatibility mode and must preserve its existing helper order, content, formatting, filename, state, and page count exactly.

## Stage rationale

### HR screen

The HR subset prioritizes concise positioning, company motivation, supplemental questions, recent intelligence, and a small proof foundation. It prepares Christian for fit, logistics, and recruiter follow-up without loading later-round analysis material.

### Hiring manager

The hiring-manager subset adds role diagnosis, ownership framing, scorecard answers, focused stories, and likely operating questions. Its purpose is to make the story bank reachable in a practical guide instead of requiring the full `all` library.

### Panel

The panel subset favors breadth: competency decoding, cross-functional delivery, the primary and additional story banks, pushback handling, and business-context questions. It supports multiple interviewers testing the same evidence from different angles.

### Presentation

The presentation subset emphasizes executive structure, delivery discipline, trust, objections, business context, and clear answer mechanics. It omits unrelated story-library depth while retaining enough proof to defend recommendations.

### Technical

The technical subset centers scenario reasoning, scorecard proof, story anchors, focused examples, keyword language, and business framing. It keeps technical answers evidence-bound rather than turning the guide into a generic reference dump.

### Final

The final subset prepares close-stage judgment: executive presence, offer blockers, recurring delivery habits, company fit, note-driven questions, closing questions, and follow-up strategy. It deliberately spans proof, motivation, risk, and conversion.

### All

`all` is the complete reference library and compatibility anchor. It remains unbounded and preserves every existing helper call in source order. Its page count is captured behavior, not a minimum, target, or quality requirement.
