# How I Think, and How to Communicate With Me

A short profile so people, and AI assistants, can deliver information the way my mind absorbs it best. Feel free to paste this at the start of a chat, share it with a manager or mentor, or keep it for yourself.

## How my mind works
- I am a **top-down, big-picture thinker**. I grasp the whole system and the *why* first, then fit the details onto that frame.
- Strong at **pattern recognition** and connecting ideas across domains.
- **Abstract and non-linear.** I operate in flows, not rigid step-by-step order.
- **Meaning-driven.** I learn fast and go deep when there is a real purpose or long-term goal attached. Without meaning, rote work drains me quickly.
- **Introverted.** Social and unstructured settings are draining; I do my best when prepared.
- I am likely autistic (RAADS-R above threshold) and navigating CPTSD. I value directness, predictability, and understanding the reason behind things.

## My strengths
- Systems thinking, architecture, strategy, and teaching complex ideas simply.
- Deep focus and fast learning when the goal matters to me.
- Honesty, depth, and loyalty to the people and ideas I commit to.

## My friction points (support needs, not flaws)
- Fine-motor and linear or rote tasks (handwriting, showing every step, busywork).
- I need the *why* to fully commit. Without it I can seem resistant, but it is buy-in, not defiance.
- Open-ended change and hard decisions are tougher; I do better with clear, reversible next steps.
- Social and sensory load; I need recovery time afterward.

## How to give me information (please follow this)
1. Lead with the **big picture and the why** before any detail.
2. Give the **answer or the point first** (bottom line up front), then support it.
3. **Connect it to the long-term goal** or higher purpose.
4. Use **patterns, analogies, and diagrams** over step-by-step lists.
5. Provide steps when I need them, but do not require me to show every intermediate step.
6. Be **direct and concise**; tell me what is essential vs optional; end with **one clear next action**.

## How to work with me best
- Explain the reason behind requests and changes.
- Give me autonomy and a meaningful goal, and I will run hard.
- Let me offload low-level, linear, and admin tasks to tools and delegation.
- Prepared, purpose-connected, and regulated, I do my best work.
