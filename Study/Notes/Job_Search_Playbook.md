# Job Search Playbook
### The three highest-leverage moves from the career-notes analysis: LinkedIn, your salary anchor, and a referral-first routine.

**Bottom line up front:** every high-earner in those videos won on the same three things, be findable, know your number, and get referred. Mass applications are the losing move (your own tracker shows the spray-to-stretch-roles pattern coming back FAIL). This playbook fixes all three. All facts below are from your documented experience; adjust anything that is not exactly right.

## 1. LinkedIn optimization (be findable weekly)

The goal from the videos: a profile good enough that recruiters message you weekly because you show up in their keyword-and-certification searches. Two rules: use concrete keywords, and cut vague soft skills.

### Headline (pick one, edit to taste)
- **Option A (implementation-forward):** "Enterprise Implementation & Solutions Consultant | ERP + SaaS delivery, data migration, and stakeholder enablement | Turning ambiguous cross-functional problems into adopted systems"
- **Option B (adds your upskilling):** "Implementation & Solutions Consultant | ERP + SaaS, data migration, SQL, stakeholder enablement | Bridging business and technology | Studying Security+, cloud, and applied AI"
- **Option C (both-sides differentiator):** "Solutions & Implementation Consultant who has delivered the same software from both sides, vendor and customer | ERP, SaaS onboarding, data migration, 80+ client configurations"

Keep it under about 220 characters. Lead with the searchable title (Implementation Consultant, Solutions Consultant), then the concrete skills.

### About section (first person, meat-first)
> I turn ambiguous, cross-functional problems into structured delivery that actually gets adopted.
>
> I have delivered the same kind of enterprise software from both sides of the table. As the vendor at Aptean, I implemented and supported 80-plus clients across the US, the Midwest, and Europe, adapting to every configuration from legacy on-premise to cloud. As the customer at East West Manufacturing, I led the end-to-end ERP implementation and data migration onto Aptean Intuitive across a five-site, 150-plus-user, global operation.
>
> Along the way I have run onboarding, compliance, financial, and training sessions; led ETL and data migration; built 200-plus dashboards; facilitated 60-plus executive workshops and QBRs; and recovered roughly a million dollars of at-risk revenue by turning churning accounts around.
>
> My best work sits at the intersection of business, operations, and technology, where most initiatives break. I am now deepening SQL, cloud (AWS and Azure), security (Security+), and applied AI so I can bridge the business and the build even better.
>
> I am open to Implementation and Solutions Consultant, Customer Success, and Technical Program Management roles. If you are working on hard delivery or adoption problems, let's connect.

### Skills to list (concrete, recruiter-searchable)
ERP Implementation, Aptean, Epicor, Data Migration, ETL, SQL, SaaS Onboarding, Solutions Consulting, Stakeholder Management, Requirements Gathering, Project Coordination, Customer Success, Process Improvement, Dashboards and Reporting, Change Management, Cloud (AWS, Azure).

**Remove the vague ones:** hardworking, problem-solver, team player, detail-oriented. Recruiters do not search those, and your own resume system bans them for the same reason.

### Profile checklist
- Photo and a simple banner; location set to Atlanta.
- Headline and About as above; turn on "Open to Work" (recruiters-only if you prefer discretion).
- Experience bullets mirror your resume, result-first.
- Add the certs you earn as you go (recruiters search them).
- Post or engage weekly (see section 3) so you keep showing up.

## 2. Your salary anchor (decide it before you interview)

The rule from the recruiter video: determine your number before you start interviewing, not during. It anchors the negotiation and stops you from underselling in the moment.

### How to set it
1. **Research the market** for your target roles in Atlanta: Levels.fyi, Glassdoor, LinkedIn Salary, Payscale, and the Robert Half Salary Guide. Filter by role, location, and seniority.
2. **Write three numbers:**
   - **Walk-away minimum** (below this, no).
   - **Target** (your realistic anchor).
   - **Stretch** (a great outcome).
3. **Anchor from your target.** When you must give a number, state a range whose bottom is your target, or your target as a single number, never your minimum.

### Fill this in (yours to decide)
- Target (anchor): $____
- Minimum (walk-away): $____
- Stretch: $____

### Negotiation rules (from the videos and standard practice)
- Do not volunteer compensation first. If a screen asks early, say you are focused on fit and are flexible for the right role, and ask their budgeted range.
- Let them make the first offer when you can; the party that anchors first often sets the range.
- Negotiate the whole package: base, bonus, equity, PTO, remote, start date.
- Silence is a tool. After they state a number, pause; you do not have to fill it.

## 3. Referral-first weekly routine (stop spraying, start connecting)

The winning pattern across every video: referrals and reputation, not application volume. Several speakers had not applied to a job in years. "You need someone to put your name in rooms you cannot enter."

### The shift
Fewer, warmer, better-targeted beats mass-apply. Your tracker confirms it: the broad applications to stretch roles keep coming back FAIL. Concentrate on Strong and Adjacent-fit roles, and add a human touch to each.

### Your weekly job-search hour (a checklist)
- **3 to 5 targeted applications** to Strong or Adjacent-fit roles only. For each, find one person at the company and send a short, specific message.
- **2 to 3 thoughtful comments** on posts from people in your target lanes (use `People_and_Lanes_to_Follow.md`). Substance, not "great post."
- **1 to 2 connection requests** with a personal note (target-company employees, former colleagues, alumni).
- **1 reconnect** with a warm contact who could refer you (former Aptean or East West colleagues are gold).
- **1 build-in-public post** (below).
- **Log it** in your tracker.

### Build in public (your brand and referral engine)
Post once a week on what you are learning or building: a SQL query you figured out, a Security+ concept, a container demo, a lesson from an implementation. This suits how you work (written, prepared), it feeds the LinkedIn presence recruiters find, and it compounds into the personal brand and referral network the videos all pointed to. It is also the first step of your longer founder and evangelist path.
