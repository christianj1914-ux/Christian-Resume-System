# 10-Minute Pre-Interview Checklist

Run this in the ten minutes before a call. Do not re-read your whole guide. This is calm plus crisp, nothing else.

## 1. Ground (30 seconds)
Say both anchors once, out loud:
- "My brain is trying to keep me safe. I am secure enough to handle this."
- "I am not selling myself. I am diagnosing their problem."

## 2. Lock your answer shape (this fixes what costs you rounds)
Every answer, no exceptions:
1. Sentence one is the answer, with your ownership verb. ("I owned," "I led," "It ended due to a layoff.")
2. One closest example. Not two. The strongest one.
3. The outcome, with a number.
4. Stop. Silence is fine. Length reads as reactive; brevity reads as senior.

## 3. Load your three questions (ask the outcome ones first)
- "What does success look like in the first three to six months for this role?"
- "Where does this role or team most often get stuck today?"
- "Who are the key stakeholders, and what does each of them care about most?"
Never answer "no, I have no questions." That alone has cost you.

## 4. Pick one signature story to land
Choose one before the call and lead it with the number:
- 78 percent less manual work, 22 percent fewer discrepancies (automation and process).
- Over 1 million dollars in at-risk revenue recovered (customer and delivery under pressure).
- Five-month, four-party EFT/ACH replacement (cross-functional project delivery).
They will repeat your number in the debrief. Give them one.

## 5. Write your company hypothesis (fill this in now)
One sentence you will say early: "From the posting, it looks like your main challenge is ______. Is that close?" Being right or slightly wrong both help; it shows you arrived thinking like an employee.

## 6. Presence reminders
- Declarative, not tentative. Cut "I guess," "kind of," "I just," and restarts.
- Let one beat of silence stand instead of filling it.
- Controlled beats energetic.

## 7. Close on purpose (last two minutes of the call)
- Name the likely objection yourself: "I am newer to your exact domain, and here is why that will not slow me down."
- State specific fit in one line.
- Ask about next steps and timeline.

Reframe to carry in: "I am not inflating anything. I am giving them accurate evidence and diagnosing their problem so they can decide well." Then answer at full size, and stop when the proof lands.
