# Interview Scorecard - Adobe: Business Architect & AI Evangelist

**Positioning move:** Lead as the business-architecture + AI-adoption person, not an AI engineer.
Your edge is turning business needs into architecture and driving AI adoption with real change
management. Gap-pivot the deep AI-engineering (RAG internals, vector DBs, agent protocols) as
learning-in-progress. Open every answer BLUF: Answer -> Example -> Result -> Relevance.

## Two anchor stories to pre-load for this role
- **Adoption + your own AI use:** "I reduced manual work ~78% at East West by redesigning and
  automating a process, and I now use Claude and Codex daily to accelerate documentation and
  analysis. I don't just evangelize AI tools, I run my own work on them." (adoption, metrics,
  AI-hands-on)
- **Business-need to architecture:** the EFT/ACH replacement, mapped current-state across IT,
  finance, vendor, bank, then one accountable delivery path. (business architecture, discovery,
  stakeholder alignment)

## 30/60/90 (say it, then ask "does that match?")
30: learn the Employee Platform, the AI toolset (Copilot, Glean, Adobe's own), and how teams work
now; map current-state and adoption baselines. 60: run first literacy workshops and stand up
adoption metrics. 90: publish a capability map + gap analysis and a prioritized AI-enabler roadmap
with measured adoption lift.

## Three questions that make you sound like the hire
- "What separates good from great at driving AI adoption here, is it the architecture rigor or the
  evangelism and change management?"
- "Where are teams stuck today, tool awareness, trust, or workflow fit?"
- "How do you currently measure AI adoption and impact, and where do you want that to go?"

## Honest gap watch (tie to your learning path)
- Deep AI (RAG/vector/agentic protocols): Track T + AI-900, in progress. Say "working knowledge,
  deepening."
- TOGAF / formal architecture artifacts: Business Architecture track. Say "I do this in practice."
- Tableau: Power BI is your strength; note Tableau ramping.
Do not claim any credential or protocol depth you do not have. Your winning frame is: the rare
person who can architect, drive adoption, AND already uses the tools, with the engineering depth
coming.
