Strategic Solutions Consultant
CreatorIQ's Strategic Solutions Consultant (SCs) are trusted technical advisors embedded in the sales cycle for our largest, most complex strategic accounts. These are top-tier global brands with layered organizations, sophisticated tech stacks, and high expectations.

Blending deep technical fluency, architectural thinking, and consultative selling, SEs partner with Sales and cross-functional teams to dissect a prospect's ecosystem from the ground up, uncovering root needs that often span multiple teams, systems, and business units. They design end-to-end solutions that map CreatorIQ's platform to real business outcomes, and they present those solutions with equal confidence to a room of API engineers, a CMO, or a procurement committee.

SCs are the product and platform experts in the room, trusted by prospects, customers, and internal stakeholders alike to cut through complexity, articulate clear business value, and guide high-stakes buying decisions to close.

 
What you'll do:

Partner with Sales to lead deep, multi-stakeholder discovery across technical, marketing, and executive audiences, uncovering the real business problems beneath surface-level requirements

Map complex organizational structures, workflows, and integration landscapes to architect tailored CreatorIQ solutions across departments and teams

Design and deliver high-impact product demonstrations, custom workshops, and white-glove trials aligned to each account's strategic objectives

Develop compelling, account-specific presentations and collateral for both technical and non-technical stakeholders

Serve as the technical authority on CreatorIQ's API capabilities (REST/GraphQL), platform architecture, data model, and integration framework

Articulate CreatorIQ's AI and machine learning capabilities, helping prospects understand how intelligent features drive measurable business outcomes

Own and drive security questionnaires, InfoSec reviews, and compliance documentation, partnering with internal Security and Legal teams to deliver thorough, timely responses

Translate discovery insights into actionable, architecture-informed recommendations for brands and campaigns

Partner with Account Executives and cross-functional teams to develop strategic, differentiated RFP/RFI/security questionnaire responses

Maintain deep expertise in CreatorIQ's platform, integrations, API surface, AI capabilities, and product roadmap

Adapt your message fluidly, from walking engineers through API documentation to presenting ROI narratives to C-suite executives

 
Who you are and what you'll need for this position:

5+ years in Solutions Consulting, Sales Engineering, or a consultative pre-sales role with meaningful time on large, complex enterprise accounts (SaaS required)

Hands-on familiarity with REST and/or GraphQL APIs with the ability to speak credibly about integrations, data flows, and technical architecture

Working knowledge of AI/ML concepts and genuine curiosity about how AI is reshaping marketing technology

Experience with MCP (Model Context Protocol), AI agents, and agentic integration patterns, with the ability to advise enterprise clients on how these capabilities fit into their technology roadmap

Experience managing security questionnaires, InfoSec reviews, and compliance processes as part of enterprise sales cycles

Demonstrated ability to navigate complex, multi-stakeholder organizations and build trust from practitioners to C-suite

Strong storyteller and presenter who translates technical architecture into clear, compelling executive-level business value

Confidently leads customer meetings independently with authority, strong presentation skills, and the ability to command the room

Naturally curious and analytical; you dig past the stated requirement to find the root problem

Confident and composed in ambiguity; you bring structure where there is none and make progress without perfect information

Experience in influencer marketing, social media, or selling to enterprise marketing/brand teams is a strong plus

Methodical in preparation, yet adaptable in execution

High ownership, accountability, and a collaborative instinct

Motivated to continuously deepen your expertise in CreatorIQ's platform and the broader industry
