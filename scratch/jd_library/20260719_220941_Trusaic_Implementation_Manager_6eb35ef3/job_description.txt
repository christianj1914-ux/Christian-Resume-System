Company: Trusaic


Job Title: Implementation Manager 


Who we are: Trusaic is a regulatory compliance software company focused on creating better work environments, for everyone. Our mission is to help organizations create authentic change, whether that be by achieving pay equity, fostering a more diverse and inclusive workforce, increasing bottom lines, or offering more affordable healthcare.


Role Overview: 

We are looking for a skilled Implementation Project Manager here at Trusaic! Our product is an enterprise software platform that addresses pay and opportunity equity with the mission of creating a better working world for all. In this role, you will be responsible for leading the delivery of client onboarding and implementation of our PayParity product.


Responsibilities:

Accountable for project planning and execution, customer experience, and ensuring Trusaic delivery meets committed scope and budget
Employ project management excellence to navigate the complex organizations of large enterprise customers
Maintain excellence of project governance with internal and customer stakeholders, managing day-to-day project planning and execution, project scope, expectations and risks, and ensuring timelines are met and quality results are produced
Hold weekly check-in calls with client teams to dialogue on current deliverables, questions, and next steps; provide updates to client on project implementation status
Hold daily scrum calls with internal project team (Client Services, Data Science, Product Management) to track progress on imminent deliverables
Co-lead weekly internal team meeting, documenting and presenting current status for all clients
Build strong relationships with our clients, acting as a trusted advisor and project manager throughout implementation


 Skills and Experience:

2-5 years of experience project managing clients through software implementation
Strong attention to detail and accuracy, managing many schedules and priorities simultaneously
Strong verbal and written communication skills and ability to engage with clients and internal workstreams
Ability to work with little guidance on complex work steps and juggle many projects at once
Ability to communicate complex statistical concepts and technical findings to non-technical audiences and participate effectively in client facing settings with varied interactions, requests, and diverse countries and cultures
Ability to modulate discussions and areas of focus given the client team’s level of experience with pay equity reviews and quickly respond to client concerns
Comfortable with ambiguity
Comfortable with leading multiple client-facing calls weekly
Familiarity with Human Resources/Compensation processes a plus
