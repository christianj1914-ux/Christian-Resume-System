Job Title: Supply Chain Operations Manager 



Supply Chain Operations Manager (Inventory and Logistics)
Atlanta, GA | Hybrid



We are seeking a Supply Chain Operations Manager to lead inventory management, warehouse operations, and logistics execution in support of a growing organization.
This role is responsible for ensuring inventory accuracy, material availability, warehouse optimization, and efficient movement of equipment to project sites. The ideal candidate combines strong inventory management expertise with operational leadership and process improvement experience.


Responsibilities
Develop and maintain inventory control processes and systems
Drive inventory accuracy, cycle counts, and inventory visibility
Partner with project, procurement, and operations teams to align material availability with project schedules
Manage warehouse operations, inventory reporting, and material movement
Coordinate logistics activities with carriers, vendors, and 3PL providers
Improve processes, implement SOPs, and identify operational efficiencies
Analyze inventory trends and provide recommendations to improve service levels while reducing excess inventory
Lead cross-functional inventory and supply chain initiatives


Preferred Background
Inventory Management
Materials Management
Supply Chain Operations
Warehouse Operations
Manufacturing or Industrial Distribution
ERP/WMS systems (SAP, Oracle, JDE, Manhattan, etc.)
Process Improvement, Lean, Continuous Improvement
