Company: Delta


Job Title: Crew Technology Product Owner



How you'll help us Keep Climbing (overview & key responsibilities)
Now is an exciting time to be part of Delta's Crew Technology team as we enhance the frontline employee experience through new decision making and execution applications.  This is an opportunity to bring new strategies and initiatives to life while retiring legacy apps.  We are seeking a Product Owner to help define the strategy, provide technical expertise, and own all current and future apps supporting tracking functions within Crew Operations. The Product Owner will work across Crew Resources, Operations Management, Flight Ops, In-Flight Service, and IT to ensure development requests are well understood across the enterprise, gain cross-divisional consensus on priorities, and ensure deliverables meet business needs.  To do so, this individual will need to manage end-to-end delivery of multiple projects at once, communicate effectively, build strong cross-divisional partnerships, understand how to write project requirements, and understand the tracker, scheduler, and crew member experience.



Summary of responsibilities (not comprehensive of all tasks):

Responsible for end-to-end product ownership and delivery duties including understanding the business problem, identifying crew member needs, and developing optimal solutions so that the business derives value from the applications
Partner with the business to deliver future strategy for back-office applications to enable transformation of crew processes
Understand the business and technology vision and translate it into strategy and requirements so that products meet the business’s needs
Work with end users (Flight Attendants, Pilots, Schedulers, Trackers, and the CRW Back Office) to understand their needs and develop product enhancements that provide value to Flight Plans and enterprise and initiative level goals
Prepare and delivery communications that include ad hoc reports, executive summaries, and formal presentations to senior leaders, so the audience understands status, what is being delivered, and the value it is adding to their specific spaces and areas
Understand FAR, FAWR, and PWA rules and deliver safe and compliant end-to-end back-office technology solutions in compliance with these rules
Prioritize product backlog, projects, and initiatives across multiple stakeholder groups so that IT is working on the right things
Work with senior leadership to deliver added value as part of initiative integration into the crew tracking and technology spaces ensuring alignment across the product, IT and business teams, while identifying opportunities to improve business processes and propose potential action plans
Build and maintain an executable product roadmap that balances business value, funding, cross-initiative dependencies, and development complexity that guides current and future development
Write features, epics, and acceptance criteria that enable a smooth handoff to development partners in IT and participate in Agile ceremonies with development teams to provide direction and drive progress
Define value of new functionality and ensure that the end product meets the needs and goals of CRW and Crew members while ensuring stakeholder alignment across the enterprise
Lead increment planning to align business priorities with available IT capacity across teams and squads
Represent CRW Technology in working groups and initiatives that require Tracking technology and business process expertise
Develop subject matter expertise for crew related data and systems that can be translated into optimal design and development
Understand the IT development space and serve as its voice in business side conversations, estimation and planning
Model Delta's Rules of the Road and core values
Practice safety-conscious behaviors in all operational processes and procedures

Verbally present ideas succinctly and persuasively; demonstrate logical thinking when responding to questions or providing alternative solutions
Ability to develop presentations that include visuals that advance the story, speak to an executive audience, and influence outcomes
Experience translating a business vision into written requirements
Track record of managing products or complex projects successfully
Ability to lead and influence others responsible for deliverables, including stakeholders from other departments
Exhibit curiosity and think creatively to help identify solutions
Proven success in understanding and managing complex processes
Willingness to become the subject matter expert for your product and take ownership for its development and performance
Self-starter with the ability to work well with diverse groups in a time sensitive and team environment with minimal supervision
Results oriented and capable of meeting or exceeding deadlines with attention to detail and follow through
Currency in user experience best practices and new technology trends
Embraces diverse people, thinking and styles
Must be performing satisfactorily in present position (if transferring internally)
What will give you a competitive edge (preferred qualifications)
Bachelor’s degree in Business, Economics, Engineering, or Technology
Experience in creation and design, or management of technology systems
Prior experience as an agile product manager/owner preferred
Broad knowledge of Crew Resources, Flight Ops, or Inflight Service preferred
