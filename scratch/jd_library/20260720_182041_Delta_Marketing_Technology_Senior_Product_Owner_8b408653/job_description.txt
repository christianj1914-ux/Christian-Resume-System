Company: Delta


Job Title: Marketing Technology Senior Product Owner




Join us as a driving force behind digital innovation, where your expertise will shape the future of Delta’s Marketing Technology and accelerate our transformation into a highly effective, data-driven, and technology-enabled organization.


As a Product Owner, you’ll work cross-functionally to tackle complex challenges, translating high-level business goals into actionable product capabilities. You’ll develop a holistic understanding of how integrated efforts across teams drive meaningful outcomes within our marketing ecosystem.

This role requires not only strong product execution, but also a working understanding of how marketing capabilities are enabled through integrated technology and data systems.


You’ll collaborate with multiple squads using agile best practices to deliver consistent, data-informed product features. Beyond execution, you’ll champion a culture of continuous improvement—identifying upskilling opportunities, enhancing team capabilities, and fueling innovation across the organization.
