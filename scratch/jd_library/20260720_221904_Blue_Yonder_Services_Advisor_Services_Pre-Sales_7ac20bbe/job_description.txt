Job title: Services Advisor (Services Pre-Sales)

Location: Virtual within the US

Overview

As a Services Advisor Pre-Sales within our CX Services Sales team, you will play a pivotal role in driving revenue growth by selling consulting, education and other strategic offerings to our clients. You will act as a trusted advisor to customers, understanding their business objectives and aligning them with our professional services offerings to maximize the value of our SaaS solutions. As a Services Advisor Pre-Sales you will be assigned Net New SaaS Attached opportunities as well as support Client Partner/Global Services Directions with SME for their sales cycles. You will partner with SaaS Sales building the plan to win and jointly go to market to win new business. 

What you'll do

Sales and Business Development:

Collaborate with Client Executives and other sales team members to co-sell services alongside software sales
Be a driving force in the SaaS sales cycle, becoming the prospects trusted advisor
Be the expert in the vertical/solution you cover on the solutions and how we implement them effectively, building a delivery program specifically for the prospect
Identify, qualify, and close opportunities for consulting, education, and other services related to our SaaS solutions
Develop strategies to increase our win rate and make Blue Yonder Services the first choice
Client Engagement:

Build and maintain strong relationships with key stakeholders, including customer executives, project managers, and technical leads
Present tailored service proposals, Statements of Work (SOWs), and pricing models aligned to client goals and budget
Build collaborative relationships that increase the customer’s value of Blue Yonder Services and expand future business opportunities
Collaborate with appropriate Blue Yonder teams on the client's behalf to deliver success and establish credibility
Demonstrate deep industry knowledge and experience within the appropriate vertical/solution area, providing advice to customers regarding best practices
Collaboration and Internal Alignment:

Partner with delivery teams to ensure services are scoped accurately, setting the stage for successful project execution
Translate customer feedback into actionable insights to inform service offering development and enhancements
Leadership and Mentorship

Serve as a mentor to more junior Client Partners, sharing best practices and supporting their professional growth
Lead by example, demonstrating a commitment to excellence, client satisfaction, and team collaboration
What we are looking for

Experience: 7+ years in professional services sales, consulting, or account management within a SaaS or technology company, with a proven track record of meeting or exceeding sales targets
Industry Experience: Deep expertise in Demand Forecasting and Supply Planning is required for this role, including the ability to speak in depth about operational processes, business challenges, and customer needs within these domains.
Domain Knowledge: Deep understanding of SaaS business models, enterprise software implementations, and the value of professional services in driving customer success
Sales Expertise: Expertise in solution selling, contract negotiation, and building long-term client relationships
Business Acumen: Ability to understand client business objectives, industry challenges, and translate them into impactful service solutions
Collaboration: Strong ability to work cross-functionally with technical, product, and sales teams
Communication: Exceptional verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to influence and present to senior executives
Preferred Qualifications

Blue Yonder Solution knowledge
Understanding of project delivery methodologies
Experience selling to enterprise-level customers
Proficiency with CRM tools (e.g., Salesforce) and sales enablement platforms
