Job Title: SaaS Implementation Manager


We're massively excited to be recruiting exclusively for a Technical Project Manager/ Implementation Manager to join a dynamic, Start Up-style team within a global, data-driven organisation. Think fast-moving, collaborative, and autonomous - no slow processes and red tape here!

You’ll lead the end-to-end delivery of the groups platform to customers, acting as the primary point of contact during onboarding and implementation. Working closely with customer success, engineering, data, and design teams, you’ll be a dab hand at coordinating internal teams, managing timelines, and ensuring clients have a seamless journey from kickoff to go-live. ✔️


The Work You’ll Own:



Lead the seamless onboarding and implementation of the platform.
Keep projects on track, managing timelines, risks, and priorities.
Coordinate technical delivery, including any customisations or data work.
Track progress, report updates, and ensure smooth collaboration across team.


Is It For You?



You’re likely someone with 5+ years of project management experience, preferably in SaaS, who thrives in client-facing roles and complex, cross-functional environments. You take ownership, proactively solve problems, and can communicate technical concepts clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
