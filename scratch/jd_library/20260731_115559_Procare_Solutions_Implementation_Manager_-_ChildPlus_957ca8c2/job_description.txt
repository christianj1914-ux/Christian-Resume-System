Company: Procare Solutions
Job Title: Implementation Manager - ChildPlus

About Procare

For over 30 years, Procare Solutions has been dedicated to empowering early childhood educators by providing products and services that enable them to focus on the care, safety and education of children. We recognize the responsibility that comes with nurturing and educating children, which is why our child care management solutions are designed to automate business processes, help ensure safety and compliance, communicate with families and provide educational resources and training to help teachers and children thrive.

Over 40,000 satisfied customers have chosen Procare Solutions as their trusted partner in providing exceptional care for young minds.

A Little About The Role

As an Implementation Manager, you will serve as the primary owner of the customer implementation experience, guiding customers from contract execution through successful onboarding, product adoption, and long-term readiness for success. You will partner closely with customers to understand their business objectives, configure solutions that align with their operational needs, and drive measurable implementation outcomes through proactive project management, consultative guidance, and education. This role requires balancing multiple customer engagements while managing timelines, mitigating risks, removing implementation barriers, and ensuring customers are positioned to realize value from their investment. Success in this role is measured by your ability to build trusted customer relationships, deliver exceptional implementation experiences, and achieve key business outcomes through strong execution and customer advocacy.

What You’ll Do

Serve as the primary point of contact and trusted advisor throughout the customer implementation lifecycle. 
Manage a portfolio of concurrent customer implementations while ensuring projects remain on schedule, within scope, and aligned with customer expectations. 
Lead customer kickoff meetings, establish implementation plans, define project milestones, and communicate timelines and responsibilities. 
Configure software solutions based on customer business requirements and implementation best practices. 
Deliver consultative training that enables customers to confidently adopt and utilize Procare solutions within their organization. 
Guide customers through change management by helping them transition existing processes into the Procare platform. 
Proactively identify implementation risks, customer concerns, and project roadblocks while developing appropriate mitigation plans. 
Monitor customer progress and engagement throughout the implementation process, taking ownership of customer outcomes and overall implementation success. 
Build strong relationships with customer stakeholders through consistent communication, transparency, and proactive follow-up. 
Collaborate with Sales, Customer Support, Technical Services, Product Management, Customer Success, and other internal teams to ensure seamless customer experiences. 
Document customer interactions, implementation progress, project milestones, and risks within company systems.
