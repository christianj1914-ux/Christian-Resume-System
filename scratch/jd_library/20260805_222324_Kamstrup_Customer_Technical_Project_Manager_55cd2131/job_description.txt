Company: Kamstrup
Job Title: Customer Technical Project Manager

Do you have experience with managing technical projects? Do you thrive in a customer-oriented role, and do you want to provide the best project experience for our customers? Would you like to become part of an international company that is a leader in intelligent solutions for energy and water?



Become a part of our US Technical Support Team



Kamstrup is a Danish technology company and one of the world’s leading manufacturers of intelligent metering solutions. Our ambition is to turn meter data into insight to help utility companies optimize their production and distribution of energy and water. Kamstrup’s water business is undergoing rapid development, which means that the requirements and demands for our smart solutions targeting the water industry are growing.



In the role of Customer Technical Project Manager your primary task will be supporting water meter deployments in a partner-led environment. This role focuses on coordinating distribution partners, supporting technical implementation, and ensuring successful integration and field execution.


RESPONSIBILITIES



Coordinate deployment activities with distribution partners, installers, and prime contractors.
Support hardware and software implementation, including meter devices and communications.
Assist with system integrations (HES, MDMS, utility platforms) and integration testing (SIT/UAT).
Provide technical support and troubleshooting for device, connectivity, and data issues.
Support radio licensing and communications setup.
Travel to customer sites for kickoffs, pilots, training, and issue resolution.
Deliver technical training to partners and customer teams.
Monitor deployment progress and escalate risks or issues as needed.
Ensure alignment with prime contractor timelines and program requirements.


PERSONAL & PROFESSIONAL QUALIFICATIONS, COMPETENCIES AND EDUCATION



2-5+ years in technical project management, utilities, or project deployments.
Experience with field deployments and CIS integrations.
Strong troubleshooting and stakeholder coordination skills.
Willingness to travel 25%.
Successful meter activation and integration.
Effective partner execution and training.
Timely issue resolution and deployment progress.
High customer and partner satisfaction.

ABOUT KAMSTRUP



Kamstrup develops, produces, and sells smart energy and water meters, relating reading systems, data analysis tools and services to utilities and other users over the entire world. Through our leadership, expertise, and passion for partnership, we always think forward in pursuit of a brighter energy future; one that advances the performance of every customer while creating lasting value for the communities they serve. We take responsibility for the way we act, and we are committed to reduce waste with the purpose of securing clean water and energy to a maximum number of people.



Kamstrup has over 1,700 employees in more than twenty countries with headquarters in Denmark. Kamstrup has healthy growth rates and a strong focus on development of technology and staff.
