Job Title: Project Manager Release Operations Ops




Paylocity is a provider of cloud-based HR and payroll software solutions, offering the most complete platform for the modern workforce. The company has become one of the fastest-growing HCM software providers worldwide by offering an intuitive, easy-to-use product suite that helps businesses automate and streamline HR and payroll processes, attract and retain talent, and build a strong workplace culture. While traditional HR and payroll providers automate basic HR processes such as payroll and benefits administration, Paylocity goes further by developing tools that HR and businesses need to compete for talent and deliver against the expectations of the modern workforce.




Position Overview 

The Project Manager Release Operations plays a key role in ensuring the seamless execution of release processes, effective communication, and training initiatives. They are responsible for ensuring that process maps are followed, frontline service teams are fully prepared for upcoming changes, and both internal and external stakeholders, including clients, are ready for operational transitions. They are highly organized, detail-oriented, and excel in coordinating cross-functional teams to drive successful change adoption and client satisfaction.





Primary Responsibilities

The below represents the primary duties of the position, others may be assigned as needed. To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each essential duty satisfactorily. The requirements listed below are representative of the knowledge, skill, and/or ability required. Reasonable accommodations may be made to enable individuals with disabilities to perform the essential functions.



Release Execution & Coordination

• Ensure that defined process maps are followed and executed effectively.

• Collaborate with Product & Technology and Operations teams to manage release activities.

• Track and report on release readiness, execution progress, and post-release outcomes.

• Identify and escalate risks, bottlenecks, and dependencies that may impact release success.



Operational & Client Readiness

• Assess the impact of upcoming releases on frontline teams and ensure they are equipped for change.

• Ensure clients are informed, trained, and prepared for upcoming system/process updates.

• Partner with Implementation, Client Services, and all other supporting teams in Operations to assess and address client needs.

• Coordinate user acceptance testing (UAT) efforts, gathering feedback from both internal teams and clients.

• Assist in developing client-facing readiness materials, including FAQs, guides, webinars, and training sessions.

• Act as a liaison between technical and non-technical teams, ensuring a seamless transition for clients.



Communication & Training

• Develop and distribute clear, concise communications regarding upcoming changes to both internal teams and clients.

• Partner with Training teams to create and deliver necessary training materials and sessions for internal and external audiences.

• Maintain up-to-date release documentation, FAQs, and support guides for both employees and clients.

• Conduct post-release evaluations to identify areas of improvement and refine future processes.
